Algebra · Grade 7

Balance-Scale Solver

An equation is a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side you must do to the other, or it tips. Undo the operations one step at a time until x stands alone.

2·x + 3 = 11

Set the equation a·x + b = c

Keep it balanced

What this teaches

Solving is undoing. The −b step cancels the add; the ÷a step cancels the multiply. Each move keeps the scale level — that's why it's fair to do it to both sides.

Data & Chance · Grade 7–8

Mean · Median · Mode Tug

Three ways to describe the “middle” of a dataset, and they move differently. Drag any dot and watch: the mean gets pulled toward the outlier, the median hardly flinches, the mode only cares about repeats.

mean = (x₁ + x₂ + … + xₙ) ÷ 5

The three averages

Edit the data

Drag the dots along the line. Try dragging one far to the right — the mean chases it, the median barely moves. That's why “average” is a loaded word.
