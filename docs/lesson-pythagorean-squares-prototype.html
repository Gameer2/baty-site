The Pythagorean Theorem

Build a square on each side of a right triangle. The two smaller squares — one on each leg — always cover exactly the same area as the one on the hypotenuse. That's not a coincidence to memorize as a² + b² = c²; it's a fact about area you can watch hold up as the triangle changes shape.
