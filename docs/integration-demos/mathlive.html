A real MathLive math field — click it, type sin(x)/x or \int_0^\pi, use the on-screen keyboard. The page reads the LaTeX, renders it with KaTeX (what you already use), and evaluates it with math.js (what you already use). This is the exact pipeline your engine pages would get.

What this shows. The math field gives you a WYSIWYG equation editor with a mobile-friendly virtual keyboard, smart-fraction rendering, and LaTeX in/out — none of which a plain <input> gives you. It drops into any existing engine page: replace the text box, read mf.getValue('latex'), and pipe it to the KaTeX render + math.js evaluate you already run. Cost: one ~120 KB script, self-hostable like your other vendor libs.
