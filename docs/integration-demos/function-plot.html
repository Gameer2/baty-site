Plotting

Desmos, basically.

Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover to read coordinates. Edit the functions in the panel — they re-render live. function-plot is the ~30 KB library behind this; compared to Plotly it's purpose-built for f(x) graphing and gives you trace/readout for free.

Try: sin(x)*exp(-x/5) · x^3-3x · 1/x · tan(x)
What this shows. The "graph f(x)" case done right — interactions students actually expect (zoom/pan/trace) with no chart-config boilerplate. A drop-in for the many engine pages that currently hand-roll a Plotly chart just to draw one or two curves. Keep Plotly for the statistics engine's multi-series dashboards; use this wherever the page is really just "draw this function."
