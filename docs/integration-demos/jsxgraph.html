A live JSXGraph construction. Grab any vertex and the side lengths, angles, area, and the circumscribed circle all recompute. This is geometry as construction — something Plotly (charts) and three.js (3D meshes) genuinely cannot do. The slider drives a function plot inside the same board to show both worlds in one canvas.

What this shows. Constructions with constraints, locuses, sliders, and live measurement — exactly what a geometry lesson or a "construct the circumcircle" exercise needs. JSXGraph is pure JS, ~140 KB, themable to your dark palette (the board here already uses your tokens). Strong candidate for Number Theory (Möbius transforms, modular geometry) and Complex Analysis (domain coloring scaffolding), not just a future geometry engine.
