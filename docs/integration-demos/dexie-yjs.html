Two panes, one truth.

Type in the left pane — the right pane updates live through a shared Yjs document. Hit "Save snapshot" and it's written to IndexedDB via Dexie, surviving a full refresh. This is the persistence + collaboration foundation the Scene Builder spec assumes: edits sync without conflicts, data survives reloads, no server round-trip required.

What this shows. Left = "you", right = "a collaborator" (same browser, one shared Y.Doc). Yjs is a CRDT, so two people editing at once merge without conflicts — that's the part that matters for a real Scene Builder where two students or a student + teacher edit the same scene. Dexie is the IndexedDB wrapper that persists snapshots offline; it scales to thousands of saved scenes where localStorage caps out at ~5 MB. Together they replace the hand-rolled serve.py note-save endpoint with something that works offline and for multiple users.
