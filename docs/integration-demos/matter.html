Physics

Drop things.
Watch them fall.

A 2D physics playground on Matter.js. Drop circles, boxes, and polygons — they collide, stack, and topple. Grab any body and drag it. This is the physics layer for math games where you need real collision and stacking rather than a hand-rolled parabola.

Gravity1.0
Drag bodies around with the mouse. Flip gravity to negative to watch everything float up. The "Shake" button applies random impulses — a quick way to break a stack.
What this shows. Real rigid-body physics — collision, friction, restitution, stacking — for free, in a ~90 KB lib. Use it in the Games lab whenever an activity needs objects that bump into each other (a balance-scale puzzle, a "how many balls fill the container" estimation game, a collision-energy demo). Pair with Phaser when you also need a game framework/scene graph; Matter is the physics alone and drops into any canvas.
