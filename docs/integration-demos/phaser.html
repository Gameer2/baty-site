Aim. Fire. Learn.

A small projectile game built on Phaser 3 — set angle and power, fire, and watch the parabola. A hit plays a sound generated at runtime through Howler. This is the shape of a Games-lab activity: a real game loop, physics, and audio, all themable to your identity.

Target moves after each shot. The trajectory is the parabola y = v·sinθ·t − ½gt² — make it land on the marker.

What this shows. A complete game loop — input → physics → render → feedback — in ~40 lines of scene code. Phaser handles the canvas, loop, and rendering; Howler handles audio (the ping here is synthesized in-browser, so no audio files to ship). For a Games lab this is the foundation: number-line jumps, projectile parabolas, function-matching arcade. Pair with Matter.js (next demo) when you need collision and stacking rather than a custom physics loop.
