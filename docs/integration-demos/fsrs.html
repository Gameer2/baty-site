What this shows. Real FSRS scheduling: each rating button shows the interval it would produce before you click (the small grey text). "Again" resets to ~10 min, "Easy" pushes the card out days or weeks. The card's state (New → Learning → Review) and lapses update live. ts-fsrs is a ~20 KB pure-JS library; you'd store each card's due/stability/difficulty alongside the lesson, and the lab tells a student "3 cards due today."
