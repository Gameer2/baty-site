Simulation

Grab it. Fling it.

A live CindyJS physics applet. Drag the teal ball and let go to give it velocity, or drag the infrared gravity well to move where things fall. This is the constraint-based, scriptable simulation layer the Scene Builder would use for school-level demos — no game-engine weight, designed for math/physics teaching.

1. Drag the teal ball and release — it keeps your fling's velocity and orbits the well.

2. Drag the infrared well to relocate gravity; the ball's path changes live.

3. Stop the ball by dragging it slowly and releasing without a flick.

The physics is real inverse-square gravity integrated by CindyJS's CindyLab engine — the same engine used in published math/physics teaching applets.

What this shows. Educational physics that's interactive by default — you drag elements and the simulation respects your input. For a Scene Builder, this is the difference between "watch a canned animation" and "manipulate the system." CindyJS is ~150 KB, no build step, scriptable in CindyScript, and renders into a plain div — it slots into a static page the same way your KaTeX/Plotly vendor libs do. Best fit: Schools/Scene Builder pendulums, springs, orbital mechanics, geometric locuses.
