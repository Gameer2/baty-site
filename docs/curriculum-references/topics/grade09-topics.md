# Grade 9 — Detailed Math Topic List (Jordan National Curriculum)

## Semester 1

### Unit 1: المتباينات الخطية — Linear Inequalities

#### Lesson 1.1: المجموعات والفترات — Sets and Intervals
- **Objective:** I express inequalities using sets and interval notation.
- **Key terms:** مجموعة (set), عنصر (element), المجموعة الخالية (empty set), المجموعة المنتهية/غير المنتهية (finite/infinite set), رمز الفترة (interval notation), اللانهاية (infinity), الفترة غير المحدودة (unbounded interval)
- **What's taught:** Reviews set notation (roster form, defining property) then introduces interval notation as a compact way to describe solution sets of inequalities — bounded intervals (open, closed, half-open) and unbounded intervals using the infinity symbol, connecting each interval to its number-line picture.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable number-line tool where students shade a region and the interval notation (and set-builder notation) updates live, and vice versa — typing an interval renders the shaded line.

#### Lesson 1.2: حل المتباينات المركّبة — Solving Compound Inequalities
- **Objective:** I express compound inequalities using intervals.
- **Key terms:** متباينة بسيطة (simple inequality), متباينة مركّبة (compound inequality), تقاطع (intersection), اتحاد (union), فترة محدودة (bounded interval)
- **What's taught:** A compound inequality combines two simple inequalities with "and" (intersection of solution sets) or "or" (union of solution sets); students solve and graph both types on the number line, e.g. modeling an acceptable water-temperature range for aquarium fish as an "and" compound inequality.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** Two overlapping draggable shaded regions on a number line that a toggle combines via intersection or union, showing the resulting compound solution set update in real time.

#### Lesson 1.3: تمثيل المتباينات الخطية بمتغيّرين بيانيًا — Graphing Linear Inequalities in Two Variables
- **Objective:** I graph a linear inequality in two variables.
- **Key terms:** المتباينة الخطية بمتغيّرين (linear inequality in two variables), منطقة الحلول الممكنة (feasible/solution region), المستقيم الحدودي (boundary line)
- **What's taught:** A linear inequality in two variables (ax+by<c, ≤, >, or ≥) has as its solution set an infinite collection of ordered pairs, graphed as a shaded half-plane bounded by a line (dashed for strict inequalities, solid otherwise); applied to a scheduling problem (assembling two types of microwaves under a weekly hour limit). Includes a GeoGebra lab extension for graphing these inequalities dynamically.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive plane where dragging the boundary line's slope/intercept or toggling strict vs. non-strict and the inequality direction live-updates the shaded feasible region, with draggable test points that show which side satisfies the inequality.

### Unit 2: العلاقات والاقترانات — Relations and Functions

#### Lesson 2.1: الاقترانات — Functions
- **Objective:** I determine the domain and range of a function.
- **Key terms:** علاقة (relation), مجال (domain), مدى (range), اقتران (function), اقتران متّصل/منفصل (continuous/discrete function), اختبار الخط الرأسي (vertical line test), الاقتران الخطي/غير الخطي (linear/non-linear function)
- **What's taught:** Distinguishes relations from functions (each input maps to exactly one output), introduces domain/range identification from mappings, tables, graphs, and equations, and the vertical line test for verifying a graph is a function; applied to a light-speed-distance function d(t)=300000t.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A function-vs-relation sorter where students drag point sets or curves and get instant vertical-line-test feedback, plus a domain/range highlighter that shades the input and output axes as a graph is traced.

#### Lesson 2.2: تفسير التمثيلات البيانية — Interpreting Graphical Representations
- **Objective:** I interpret graphical representations of relationships (e.g. distance/time or position/time graphs).
- **Key terms:** منحنيات التحويل (conversion graphs), منحنى الموقع-الزمن (position-time graph)
- **What's taught:** Extends earlier unit-conversion work using conversion-graph curves, and teaches reading qualitative story information (motion, rest, direction change) directly off position-vs-time graphs, e.g. determining how long a car trip lasted and how long the car was stopped from a graph shape.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A "graph storyteller" where students drag a marker along a position-time curve and watch an animated car move correspondingly, reinforcing that slope segments correspond to motion phases (constant speed, stopped, reversing).

#### Lesson 2.3: الاقتران التربيعي — The Quadratic Function
- **Objective:** I graph a quadratic function in the coordinate plane.
- **Key terms:** الاقتران التربيعي (quadratic function), الصورة القياسية (standard form), الاقتران الرئيس (parent function), قطع مكافئ (parabola), محور التماثل (axis of symmetry), الرأس (vertex), نقطة القيمة الصغرى/العظمى (minimum/maximum point)
- **What's taught:** Introduces the parabola shape of quadratic functions, standard form f(x)=ax²+bx+c, how to find the axis of symmetry, vertex, and min/max point, and reading these features off a graph; applied to modeling a bridge's arch-shaped support and checking ship clearance underneath.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** Sliders for a, b, c on f(x)=ax²+bx+c that redraw the parabola live while a marker locks onto and labels the vertex and axis of symmetry as coefficients change.

#### Lesson 2.4: التحويلات الهندسية للاقترانات التربيعية — Geometric Transformations of Quadratic Functions
- **Objective:** I graph curves of quadratic functions resulting from applying one or more geometric transformations to the parent function's graph.
- **Key terms:** التحويل الهندسي (geometric transformation), الانسحاب الرأسي/الأفقي (vertical/horizontal translation), التمدّد (stretch), الانعكاس (reflection), صيغة الرأس (vertex form)
- **What's taught:** Shows how changing the parent function f(x)=x² by translations (up/down, left/right), reflections, and stretches produces the family of quadratic curves in vertex form, comparing g(x)=x²−2 to f(x)=x² as an introductory example. Includes a GeoGebra lab exploring these transformations interactively.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** Independent sliders for vertical shift, horizontal shift, stretch factor, and a reflection toggle applied to y=x², animating the transformation step-by-step and displaying the resulting vertex-form equation.

### Unit 3: حل المعادلات — Solving Equations

#### Lesson 3.1: حل المعادلات التربيعية بيانيًا — Solving Quadratic Equations Graphically
- **Objective:** I solve a quadratic equation graphically.
- **Key terms:** المعادلة التربيعية (quadratic equation), جذور المعادلة (roots of the equation), أصفار الاقتران (zeros of the function)
- **What's taught:** Links a quadratic equation ax²+bx+c=0 to its associated quadratic function f(x)=ax²+bx+c, and shows that the equation's roots are the x-intercepts (zeros) of the function's graph; applied to finding how long a dolphin stays above water using h(t)=−5t²+10t.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A parabola grapher where dragging the curve (via coefficient sliders) highlights its x-axis crossings live and labels them as the equation's roots, reinforcing the graph-to-equation link.

#### Lesson 3.2: حل المعادلات التربيعية بالتحليل — Solving Quadratic Equations by Factoring
- **Objective:** I solve quadratic equations algebraically by factoring.
- **Key terms:** خاصية الضرب الصفري (zero-product property)
- **What's taught:** Introduces the zero-product property (if a product of factors equals zero, at least one factor must be zero) as the algebraic tool for solving factorable quadratics, moving from the previous lesson's graphical method to an algebraic one; applied to a kangaroo-jump height model h(t)=−16t²+7t.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A factoring workbench where students drag terms to build the factored form and the tool visually confirms each factor's zero against the graph's x-intercepts from lesson 3.1.

#### Lesson 3.3: حل المعادلات التربيعية بإكمال المربّع — Solving Quadratic Equations by Completing the Square
- **Objective:** I solve quadratic equations by completing the square.
- **Key terms:** إكمال المربّع (completing the square)
- **What's taught:** Builds on solving (x+m)²=n by square roots, showing that x²+2mx+m² is the expanded form of the perfect square (x+m)², so any quadratic can be rewritten in that form by adding a constructed term, then solved by taking square roots; applied to a projectile height model h(t)=−5t²+8t+1.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A geometric "completing the square" area-model animation where a rectangle representing x²+2mx is physically rearranged and a small square of area m² is added to complete a perfect square, tying the algebra to a visual proof.

#### Lesson 3.4: حل المعادلة التربيعية باستعمال القانون العام — Solving Quadratic Equations Using the Quadratic Formula
- **Objective:** I solve a quadratic equation using the general (quadratic) formula.
- **Key terms:** القانون العام (the quadratic formula), المميّز (the discriminant)
- **What's taught:** Derives the quadratic formula from the completing-the-square method applied to the general form ax²+bx+c=0, then applies it directly to any quadratic without factoring; applied to a disc-throwing game modeled by f(x)=−0.04x²+0.84x+2, solving for horizontal landing distance. Introduces the discriminant as a way to predict the number of real solutions.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A formula calculator where entering a, b, c auto-plugs into the quadratic formula step-by-step (discriminant computed first, then both roots), paired with a live parabola showing the roots as intercepts and how the discriminant sign predicts 0, 1, or 2 intersections.

### Unit 4: الهندسة الإحداثية — Coordinate Geometry

#### Lesson 4.1: المسافة في المستوى الإحداثي — Distance in the Coordinate Plane
- **Objective:** I find the midpoint of a line segment in the coordinate plane.
- **Key terms:** المسافة (distance), الإحداثي (coordinate), نقطة المنتصف (midpoint)
- **What's taught:** Reviews the distance formula between two points (derived from the Pythagorean theorem applied to coordinates) and introduces the midpoint formula, averaging x- and y-coordinates; applied to determining whether a high-resolution camera can photograph a seagull within its range on a coordinate grid, and finding a city midpoint on a map with GeoGebra.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable two-point coordinate-plane tool that draws the connecting segment, right-triangle legs, and live-computes both distance (via Pythagorean overlay) and midpoint as either point moves.

#### Lesson 4.2: البعد بين نقطة ومستقيم — Distance Between a Point and a Line
- **Objective:** I find the distance between two parallel lines.
- **Key terms:** (builds on) البعد بين نقطة ومستقيم (distance between a point and a line)
- **What's taught:** Defines the distance from a point to a line as the length of the perpendicular segment from that point to the line (the shortest such distance), then extends this to finding the distance between two parallel lines by dropping a perpendicular between them; applied to finding the shortest jump distance across a stream modeled by two points/lines.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive plane where dragging a point off a line auto-draws candidate segments to the line and highlights the perpendicular one as shortest, with a numeric distance readout, extendable to two parallel lines.

## Semester 2

### Unit 5: العلاقات في المثلّثات والنسب المثلّثية — Relationships in Triangles and Trigonometric Ratios

#### Lesson 5.1: الأجزاء المتناسبة في المثلّثات — Proportional Parts in Triangles
- **Objective:** I identify proportional parts in a triangle and use them to find unknown measures.
- **Key terms:** القطعة المنصّفة في المثلث (triangle midsegment/bisecting segment)
- **What's taught:** Uses similar-triangle relationships (from earlier grades) to show that a segment parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally, and applies this to solve for unknown side lengths. Includes a GeoGebra lab extension exploring the "midsegment triangle."
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable triangle with a parallel cross-segment where sliding the segment's position live-updates the proportional side ratios and unknown-length calculations.

#### Lesson 5.2: منصّفات في المثلّث — Bisectors in a Triangle
- **Objective:** I identify angle bisectors, perpendicular bisectors, and their special points in a triangle.
- **Key terms:** المنصّف العمودي (perpendicular bisector), مركز الدائرة الخارجية للمثلث (circumcenter), مركز الدائرة الداخلية للمثلث (incenter)
- **What's taught:** Constructs perpendicular bisectors and angle bisectors of a triangle's sides/angles, showing they are concurrent at the circumcenter (equidistant from vertices, center of the circumscribed circle) and incenter (equidistant from sides, center of the inscribed circle) respectively.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A triangle where toggling perpendicular-bisector or angle-bisector construction on each side animates the concurrency point forming, then auto-draws the circumscribed or inscribed circle centered there.

#### Lesson 5.3: القطع المتوسّطة والارتفاعات في المثلّث — Medians and Altitudes in a Triangle
- **Objective:** I identify medians and altitudes in a triangle and their special points.
- **Key terms:** القطعة المتوسّطة للمثلث (median), مركز المثلث (centroid), ارتفاع المثلث (altitude), ملتقى الارتفاعات (orthocenter)
- **What's taught:** Defines medians (vertex to opposite midpoint, concurrent at the centroid, the triangle's balance point) and altitudes (vertex perpendicular to opposite side, concurrent at the orthocenter), distinguishing both from the bisectors of lesson 5.2. Includes a concept activity on medians.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A triangle-vertex-dragging tool that simultaneously animates medians converging at the centroid and altitudes converging at the orthocenter, letting students see how these points move (and can leave the triangle) as its shape changes.

#### Lesson 5.4: النسب المثلّثية — Trigonometric Ratios
- **Objective:** I identify sine, cosine, and tangent of an acute angle as ratios between the sides of a right triangle.
- **Key terms:** النسب المثلّثية (trigonometric ratios), الجيب (sine), جيب التمام (cosine), الظل (tangent), معكوس النسبة المثلّثية (inverse trig ratio), متطابقة فيثاغورس (Pythagorean identity)
- **What's taught:** Defines sin, cos, tan of an acute angle in a right triangle as ratios of opposite/hypotenuse, adjacent/hypotenuse, and opposite/adjacent respectively; introduces inverse trig ratios to find angle measures and the Pythagorean identity sin²θ+cos²θ=1. Preceded by a concept activity introducing trig ratios.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable right triangle with a fixed hypotenuse where changing the acute angle live-updates the three side ratios as numeric sin/cos/tan values, visually reinforcing which sides pair with which ratio.

#### Lesson 5.5: تطبيقات النسب المثلّثية — Applications of Trigonometric Ratios
- **Objective:** I use trigonometric ratios to find unknown measures in a right triangle.
- **Key terms:** زاوية الارتفاع (angle of elevation), زاوية الانخفاض (angle of depression)
- **What's taught:** Applies sine, cosine, tangent, and their inverses to real-world right-triangle problems involving angles of elevation and depression — e.g. finding heights or distances that can't be measured directly, such as building heights or distances to distant objects.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A scenario simulator (e.g. flagpole or cliff) where dragging an observer's position changes the angle of elevation/depression live and shows the trig calculation solving for the unknown height or distance.

### Unit 6: المقادير الأُسّية والمقادير الجذرية — Exponential and Radical Expressions

#### Lesson 6.1: تبسيط المقادير الأُسّية — Simplifying Exponential Expressions
- **Objective:** I use properties of integer exponents to simplify exponential expressions.
- **What's taught:** Reviews and extends the laws of integer exponents (product, quotient, power, zero, and negative exponent rules) from earlier grades to simplify more complex exponential expressions, including those with variables. Includes a unit project connecting solids, exponential, and radical expressions.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An exponent-rule matching tool where dragging expressions onto the correct rule (e.g. product-of-powers, power-of-a-power) animates the simplification step and shows the resulting simplified exponent.

#### Lesson 6.2: العمليات على المقادير الجذرية — Operations on Radical Expressions
- **Objective:** I perform operations on radical expressions.
- **Key terms:** المقادير الجذرية (radical expressions), إنطاق المقام (rationalizing the denominator), المرافق (conjugate)
- **What's taught:** Covers adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing radical (root) expressions, including simplifying radicals and rationalizing denominators using conjugates; applied to finding a triangle's height given its area, expressed as a radical.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A step-by-step radical simplifier where students drag factors out from under a root symbol, with a rationalizing-the-denominator mode that shows multiplying by the conjugate to clear the radical from the denominator.

#### Lesson 6.3: حلّ المعادلات الجذرية — Solving Radical Equations
- **Objective:** I solve equations containing radical expressions.
- **Key terms:** المعادلات الجذرية (radical equations), الحلول الدخيلة (extraneous solutions)
- **What's taught:** Solves equations containing square roots by isolating the radical and squaring both sides, then stresses checking all solutions in the original equation to discard extraneous solutions introduced by squaring.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A solver that shows both candidate solutions plotted against the original radical function's graph, visually flagging which solution is extraneous (doesn't actually lie on the curve).

### Unit 7: المقادير الجبرية النسبية — Rational Algebraic Expressions

#### Lesson 7.1: ضرب المقادير الجبرية النسبية وقسمتها — Multiplying and Dividing Rational Algebraic Expressions
- **Objective:** I multiply and divide rational algebraic expressions.
- **Key terms:** المقدار الجبري النسبي (rational algebraic expression), الكسر الجبري المركّب (complex algebraic fraction)
- **What's taught:** Extends fraction multiplication/division rules (multiply straight across; divide by multiplying by the reciprocal) to algebraic fractions, requiring factoring numerators/denominators first to simplify common factors; applied to a computer-screen rectangle-dimension problem.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A factor-and-cancel animation where matching factors in numerator and denominator visually "cancel" when a student drags them together, building intuition for why simplification works.

#### Lesson 7.2: جمع المقادير الجبرية النسبية وطرحها — Adding and Subtracting Rational Algebraic Expressions
- **Objective:** I add and subtract rational algebraic expressions.
- **What's taught:** Extends fraction addition/subtraction (finding a common denominator, typically the LCD of the factored denominators) to algebraic fractions; applied to finding the glass surface area of an open-top fish tank expressed in terms of x.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A common-denominator builder where dragging factors into a shared denominator "slot" shows both fractions being rescaled simultaneously before combining.

#### Lesson 7.3: حلّ المعادلات النسبية — Solving Rational Equations
- **Objective:** I solve rational equations.
- **Key terms:** المعادلة النسبية (rational equation)
- **What's taught:** Solves equations containing rational (fractional algebraic) expressions by clearing denominators (multiplying through by the LCD), then checks for restricted values that would make any original denominator zero (excluded/extraneous solutions).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A rational-equation solver highlighting excluded domain values (where a denominator is zero) directly on a number line alongside the computed solution set, showing why those values must be rejected.

### Unit 8: الإحصاء والاحتمالات — Statistics and Probability

#### Lesson 8.1: مقاييس التشتّت — Measures of Dispersion
- **Objective:** I find and interpret the variance and standard deviation of a data set.
- **Key terms:** التباين (variance), الانحراف المعياري (standard deviation), تحويل البيانات (data transformation)
- **What's taught:** Introduces variance and standard deviation as measures of how spread out data is around the mean, building on the earlier interquartile-range measure from grade 8, and examines how shifting or scaling all data values (transformation) affects these measures.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A live dot-plot where dragging individual data points recomputes and visually redraws the mean and a standard-deviation band around it, showing spread shrink/grow in real time.

#### Lesson 8.2: الجداول التكرارية ذات الفئات — Grouped Frequency Tables
- **Objective:** I estimate measures of central tendency for grouped frequency tables.
- **What's taught:** Covers organizing continuous data into class intervals within a grouped frequency table, and estimating mean, median, and mode from grouped (rather than raw individual) data using midpoint approximations, applied to timing children completing a puzzle.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A raw-data-to-grouped-table builder where students choose class-interval width and watch the frequency table and estimated central-tendency measures recompute live as the width changes.

#### Lesson 8.3: المدرّجات التكرارية — Frequency Histograms
- **Objective:** I represent continuous grouped data in frequency histograms.
- **Key terms:** المدرّجات التكرارية (frequency histograms), الكثافة التكرارية (frequency density)
- **What's taught:** Builds histograms for continuous grouped data with unequal class widths, introducing frequency density (frequency ÷ class width) as the bar-height measure so that bar area (not height) represents frequency correctly.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A histogram builder where changing class-interval widths automatically recalculates frequency density and bar heights, visually demonstrating why density (not raw frequency) must be used for unequal-width bars.

#### Lesson 8.4: الاحتمالات وأشكال ڤن — Probability and Venn Diagrams
- **Objective:** I find probability using Venn diagrams.
- **Key terms:** الحادث المتمّم (complementary event), الحوادث المتنافية (mutually exclusive events), الحوادث الشاملة (exhaustive events)
- **What's taught:** Uses Venn diagrams to represent sample spaces and events, computing probabilities of complements, unions, and intersections, and distinguishing mutually exclusive from overlapping events, and exhaustive event sets that cover the whole sample space.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive two-circle Venn diagram where students drag outcomes into regions (union, intersection, complement) and the tool live-computes each region's probability from the counts placed.

#### Lesson 8.5: الاحتمال الهندسي — Geometric Probability
- **Objective:** I find geometric probabilities using lengths, areas, and angles.
- **Key terms:** الاحتمالات الهندسية (geometric probabilities)
- **What's taught:** Extends probability beyond counting discrete outcomes to continuous geometric measures — computing the probability of a randomly chosen point/dart landing in a target region as the ratio of the target's length, area, or angle measure to the total length, area, or angle measure available.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A dartboard-style random-point simulator where students design target regions of chosen area/angle and compare the theoretical geometric probability to the empirical hit-rate from many simulated random throws.
