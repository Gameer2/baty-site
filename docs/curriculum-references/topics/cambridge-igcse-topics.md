# Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) — Detailed Topic List

Source: Cambridge IGCSE™ Mathematics 0580 syllabus, for exams in 2025, 2026 and 2027 (Cambridge International). This is an internationally-taught qualification typically for learners aged 14–16 (roughly equivalent to Jordan grades 9–10), taken by candidates on one of two tiers:

- **Core** — grades C to G, papers 1 and 3.
- **Extended** — grades A* to E, papers 2 and 4. Extended content includes all Core content plus additional material.

Unlike the Jordan NCCD textbooks (which are narrative lesson books with worked examples), this source is an **exam syllabus/specification**: it defines exactly what skills and content candidates must know, without worked examples or teaching narrative. Entries below are organized by the syllabus's own 9 content strands and numbered sub-topics (matching the official C#.# / E#.# numbering), with **Tier** noting whether a sub-topic is Core+Extended or Extended-only, and "What's taught" expanding the syllabus's terse skill bullets into fuller descriptions.

## Strand 1: Number

#### 1.1 Types of Number
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Identifying and using natural numbers, integers (positive/zero/negative), prime numbers, square and cube numbers, common factors and multiples (HCF/LCM), rational and irrational numbers, and reciprocals; expressing a number as a product of its prime factors and converting between numbers and words.
- **Key terms:** natural numbers, integers, prime numbers, square/cube numbers, HCF, LCM, rational/irrational numbers, reciprocal
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A number-classifier tool where dragging a number onto category bins (prime, square, rational, etc.) gives instant feedback, plus a factor-tree builder for HCF/LCM.

#### 1.2 Sets
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Set language and notation (n(A), complement A′, universal set, union ∪, intersection ∩) and using two-set Venn diagrams to represent and solve problems about set membership.
- **Key terms:** set, element, universal set, complement, union, intersection, Venn diagram
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive two-circle Venn diagram where dragging elements into regions live-updates n(A), n(A∩B), n(A∪B), and A′ counts.

#### 1.3 Powers and Roots
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots, and other powers/roots of numbers; recall of squares 1–15 and cubes of 1,2,3,4,5,10 and their roots.
- **Key terms:** square, square root, cube, cube root
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A grid-based visual proof tool where dragging a square's side length live-shows the area (square number) and an inverse mode reveals the root.

#### 1.4 Fractions, Decimals and Percentages
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Using and converting between proper/improper fractions, mixed numbers, decimals, and percentages, recognizing equivalence, and writing fractions in simplest form.
- **Key terms:** proper/improper fraction, mixed number, decimal, percentage, equivalence
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A triple-linked slider (fraction bar / decimal number line / percentage bar) where changing one representation live-updates the other two.

#### 1.5 Ordering
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Ordering quantities by magnitude and using the symbols =, ≠, >, <, ⩾, ⩽.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable number line where students place values (including fractions/decimals/negatives) in order and the tool auto-checks correct ordering.

#### 1.6 The Four Operations
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Applying addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to integers, fractions, and decimals with correct order of operations and use of brackets, including negative numbers and practical contexts like temperature change.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A calculation-order animator (BIDMAS/PEMDAS) that highlights which operation executes next as an expression is evaluated step by step.

#### 1.7 Indices I
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Understanding and using indices with positive, zero, and negative integer exponents, and applying the laws of indices to evaluate and simplify expressions.
- **Key terms:** index/exponent, index laws
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An index-law matcher where dragging expressions like 2⁻³×2⁴ onto the matching rule animates the simplification.

#### 1.8 Standard Form
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Using standard form A×10ⁿ (1⩽A<10), converting numbers into and out of standard form, and calculating with values in standard form.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A magnitude slider where dragging across a huge numeric range (e.g. from atomic to astronomical scale) live-displays the number in both ordinary and standard form.

#### 1.9 Estimation
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Rounding to a specified degree of accuracy (decimal places, significant figures), making estimates for calculations, and rounding answers to a reasonable accuracy given a problem's context.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A rounding tool where a number highlighted at different place-value digits shows live how the value rounds at each significant-figure or decimal-place level.

#### 1.10 Limits of Accuracy
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Giving upper and lower bounds for data that has been rounded to a specified accuracy (e.g. a length measured to the nearest metre).
- **Key terms:** upper bound, lower bound
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A measurement-uncertainty slider showing a rounded value's true possible range as a shaded interval that narrows/widens with the rounding precision chosen.

#### 1.11 Ratio and Proportion
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Simplifying ratios, dividing a quantity in a given ratio, and using proportional reasoning in contexts like adapting recipes, map scales, and best-value comparisons.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A quantity-splitting bar where dragging a divider live-splits a total according to a ratio, with the numeric split updating in real time.

#### 1.12 Rates
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Using common measures of rate (hourly pay, currency exchange, flow rates, fuel consumption) and other rate measures (pressure, density, population density), and solving average-speed problems using the speed/distance/time relationship.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A speed-distance-time triangle calculator paired with an animated moving object whose speed can be dragged to see distance covered over time.

#### 1.13 Percentages
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating a percentage of a quantity, expressing one quantity as a percentage of another, calculating percentage increase/decrease, and simple and compound interest, including contexts like deposit, discount, and profit/loss.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A compound-interest growth simulator where adjusting rate/time/principal live-redraws the balance growth curve versus simple interest for comparison.

#### 1.14 Using a Calculator
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Efficient calculator use — entering values correctly (e.g. time as decimal hours), interpreting calculator displays correctly in context (money, time), and avoiding premature rounding mid-calculation.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A "common calculator mistakes" simulator showing side-by-side correct vs. incorrect input/rounding for the same problem.

#### 1.15 Time
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating with time units (seconds through years) and their relationships, converting between 24-hour and 12-hour clock notation, and reading clocks/timetables, including time-zone and local-time-difference problems.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive world-clock/timetable tool where dragging a departure time and time zone live-computes arrival times across zones.

#### 1.16 Money
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating with money and converting between currencies using exchange rates.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A live currency converter where entering an amount and exchange rate shows the conversion calculation step by step.

#### 1.17 Exponential Growth and Decay
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Using exponential growth and decay to model real situations such as depreciation of value or population change over time.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A growth/decay simulator with an adjustable rate slider animating a population or asset value curve over time, contrasting growth vs. decay shapes.

#### 1.18 Surds
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Understanding and simplifying surd expressions (e.g. √20=2√5) and rationalizing denominators (including with conjugate binomial denominators).
- **Key terms:** surd, rationalize the denominator
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A step-by-step surd simplifier where students drag perfect-square factors out from under the root, and a rationalizing mode animates multiplying by the conjugate.

## Strand 2: Algebra and Graphs

#### 2.1 Introduction to Algebra
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Understanding that letters represent generalized numbers, and substituting numbers into expressions and formulas.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A formula "plug-and-play" calculator where changing an input variable's slider live-recomputes the expression's value.

#### 2.2 Algebraic Manipulation
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds more factoring forms and completing the square)
- **What's taught:** Simplifying by collecting like terms, expanding products of algebraic expressions (including three-bracket products at Extended), and factorising (common factors at Core; also difference-of-two-squares, perfect-square trinomials, quadratic trinomials ax²+bx+c, and completing the square at Extended).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A tile-based algebra-tiles simulator for expanding brackets and factorising visually, extended to a "complete the square" geometric animation at the Extended level.

#### 2.3 Algebraic Fractions
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Manipulating algebraic fractions — adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing rational expressions, and factorising/simplifying rational expressions such as (x²−2x)/(x²−5x+6).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A factor-and-cancel animation (as in the Jordan grade 9 rational-expressions lesson) showing matching factors cancel between numerator and denominator.

#### 2.4 Indices II
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds fractional indices and equation solving)
- **What's taught:** Understanding and using indices (positive/zero/negative at Core; also fractional at Extended) and the laws of indices to simplify expressions and solve index equations like 3^(2x)=2.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An index-equation solver where students manipulate exponent rules step by step to isolate the variable, with each rule application animated.

#### 2.5 Equations
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds fractional/simultaneous non-linear/quadratic equations and formula rearrangement with repeated/powered subjects)
- **What's taught:** Constructing and solving linear equations in one unknown and simultaneous linear equations (both tiers); at Extended, also solving fractional equations, simultaneous equations with one linear and one non-linear equation, and quadratic equations by factorisation, completing the square, and the quadratic formula; changing the subject of formulas (simple at Core, with repeated/powered subjects at Extended).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An equation-solving step tracker showing each algebraic operation applied to both sides, extendable to a graphical intersection view for simultaneous linear/non-linear systems.

#### 2.6 Inequalities
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds solving linear inequalities and two-variable graphical regions)
- **What's taught:** Representing/interpreting inequalities on a number line at Core; at Extended, also constructing and solving linear inequalities, graphing two-variable linear inequalities (solid/broken boundary lines, shading unwanted regions), and listing inequalities defining a given region.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable region-builder plane where students shade multiple inequality regions and the overlapping feasible region highlights automatically.

#### 2.7 Sequences
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds exponential sequences and combinations)
- **What's taught:** Continuing sequences, recognizing term-to-term rules, and finding the nth term of linear, quadratic, and cubic sequences at Core; Extended adds exponential sequences and simple combinations of sequence types.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A sequence-difference table (as in the Jordan grade 10 sequences lesson) that reveals whether a sequence is linear/quadratic/cubic/exponential from its difference pattern.

#### 2.8 Proportion
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Expressing direct and inverse proportion algebraically (linear, square, square-root, cube, cube-root proportion) using the ∝ symbol, and using this to find unknown quantities.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A proportion-type explorer where switching between direct/inverse/square/cube proportion models redraws the corresponding y-vs-x curve live.

#### 2.9 Graphs in Practical Situations
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds kinematics — speed-time graphs, acceleration, area-under-graph)
- **What's taught:** Interpreting and drawing travel graphs and conversion graphs at Core; Extended adds applying rate-of-change ideas to distance-time and speed-time graphs (including acceleration/deceleration) and computing distance traveled as area under a speed-time graph.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A synced dual-graph animation (position-time and speed-time) where dragging a point on one graph animates a moving object and updates the other graph simultaneously.

#### 2.10 Graphs of Functions
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended covers a much wider function family: powers −2 to 3 and exponential terms)
- **What's taught:** Constructing tables and drawing/interpreting graphs of linear, simple quadratic, and reciprocal functions at Core; Extended broadens this to sums of power functions (exponents −2,−1,−½,0,½,1,2,3) and exponential functions abˣ+c, plus graphing exponential growth/decay and solving equations graphically (including line-curve intersections).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A function-family grapher with sliders for each term's coefficient, showing the combined curve update live as terms are added or removed.

#### 2.11 Sketching Curves
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds cubic, reciprocal, exponential families, turning points, asymptotes)
- **What's taught:** Sketching linear and quadratic graphs with symmetry/roots at Core; Extended adds cubic, reciprocal, and exponential curve families, requiring knowledge of turning points (via completing the square), roots, symmetry, and asymptotes.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A curve-sketch matching game where students match algebraic function forms to their correct sketch shape, with instant reveal of the accurate plotted curve.

#### 2.12 Differentiation
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Estimating gradients by drawing tangents, using derivatives of functions of the form axⁿ (n a non-negative integer) and sums of up to three such terms, applying differentiation to find gradients and stationary points, and distinguishing maxima from minima (via second differential or gradient inspection either side of a turning point).
- **Key terms:** derivative, dy/dx notation, stationary point, maximum, minimum
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A tangent-line dragger along a curve showing the live gradient value plotted as a point on the derivative graph, paired with a turning-point classifier highlighting sign changes either side of a critical point.

#### 2.13 Functions
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Understanding functions, domain, range, and function notation; finding inverse functions f⁻¹(x); forming composite functions gf(x)=g(f(x)).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A two-stage "function machine" for composition (as in the Jordan grade 10 composition lesson) plus an inverse-function mode reflecting a function's graph across y=x.

## Strand 3: Coordinate Geometry

#### 3.1 Coordinates
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Using and interpreting Cartesian coordinates in two dimensions.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable-point coordinate plane with live coordinate readout as points are moved.

#### 3.2 Drawing Linear Graphs
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Drawing straight-line graphs from linear equations given in the form y=mx+c or from a table of values.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A table-of-values-to-graph plotter where entering x-values auto-computes y and plots points that connect into the line.

#### 3.3 Gradient of Linear Graphs
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds computing gradient directly from two coordinate points)
- **What's taught:** Finding the gradient of a straight line from a grid at Core; Extended adds calculating the gradient algebraically from the coordinates of two points.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable two-point line where the rise/run triangle animates between the points and the gradient calculation updates live.

#### 3.4 Length and Midpoint
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Calculating the length of a line segment (distance formula) and finding the coordinates of its midpoint.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable two-point segment tool auto-displaying the right-triangle distance construction and midpoint marker, as in the Jordan grade 9 coordinate geometry lesson.

#### 3.5 Equations of Linear Graphs
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended uses more general line forms like ax+by=c)
- **What's taught:** Interpreting and obtaining the equation of a straight-line graph in the form y=mx+c (Core) or more general forms including ax+by=c (Extended), finding gradient/intercept from an equation or vice versa.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A line-equation builder where dragging the line on a grid live-updates its equation in multiple forms (slope-intercept, standard form).

#### 3.6 Parallel Lines
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Finding the gradient and equation of a line parallel to a given line, through a specified point.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable reference line with a movable point where a parallel line auto-draws through that point, gradient locked equal to the reference.

#### 3.7 Perpendicular Lines
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Finding the gradient and equation of a line perpendicular to a given line, including perpendicular bisectors of a segment joining two points.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A perpendicular-line constructor showing the negative-reciprocal gradient relationship live as one line is rotated, plus a perpendicular-bisector mode for two draggable endpoints.

## Strand 4: Geometry

#### 4.1 Geometrical Terms
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds "plane," "perpendicular bisector," "hemisphere," "frustum," major/minor arc)
- **What's taught:** Vocabulary for points, lines, angle types, similarity/congruence, scale factor, triangle/quadrilateral/polygon classification, nets, solids (cube, cuboid, prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone, sphere — plus hemisphere and frustum at Extended), and circle terminology (centre, radius, diameter, chord, tangent, arc, sector, segment).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive shape glossary where clicking any labeled part of a solid or circle diagram highlights and defines that term.

#### 4.2 Geometrical Constructions
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Measuring and drawing lines/angles, constructing a triangle from three given side lengths using ruler and compasses, and drawing/using/interpreting nets (including using net measurements to calculate volume and surface area).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A compass-and-straightedge simulator for triangle construction with visible construction arcs, plus a net-folding animation turning a 2D net into its 3D solid.

#### 4.3 Scale Drawings
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Drawing and interpreting scale drawings, and using three-figure bearings measured clockwise from north (000°–360°), including finding a reverse bearing.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A compass-rose bearing tool (shared design with the Jordan grade 10 bearing lesson) computing forward and reverse bearings as a direction line is dragged.

#### 4.4 Similarity
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds area/volume scale-factor relationships and similarity proofs)
- **What's taught:** Calculating lengths of similar shapes at Core; Extended adds using length²/area and length³/volume scale-factor relationships for similar shapes/solids, and giving geometric reasons that two shapes are similar.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A similar-solids scaler where dragging a linear scale-factor slider live-shows how area scales by its square and volume by its cube.

#### 4.5 Symmetry
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds 3D symmetry of prisms/cylinders/pyramids/cones)
- **What's taught:** Recognizing line symmetry and rotational symmetry order in 2D shapes; Extended adds symmetry properties (planes and axes of symmetry) of 3D solids.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A shape-rotator showing symmetry lines/rotational order overlay live on 2D shapes, extended to a rotatable 3D solid revealing its planes of symmetry.

#### 4.6 Angles
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds irregular polygon angle properties)
- **What's taught:** Calculating unknown angles using angle-at-a-point, angles on a line, vertically opposite angles, triangle/quadrilateral angle sums, parallel-line angle relationships (corresponding, alternate, co-interior), and regular (and irregular at Extended) polygon angle properties, with correct geometric reasoning/notation.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable parallel-line transversal where each angle pair (corresponding, alternate, co-interior) highlights and labels its relationship live as the transversal angle changes.

#### 4.7 Circle Theorems I
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Core: only semicircle angle and tangent-radius; Extended adds central/inscribed angle relationship, same-segment angles, cyclic quadrilateral, alternate segment theorem)
- **What's taught:** Angle in a semicircle = 90° and angle between tangent and radius = 90° at Core; Extended adds central angle = 2× inscribed angle on the same arc, equal angles in the same segment, cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles summing to 180°, and the alternate segment theorem.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** An interactive circle-theorem explorer (extending the Jordan grade 10 circle-angles lesson) where dragging points around the circumference keeps each theorem's angle relationship visibly constant.

#### 4.8 Circle Theorems II
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Symmetry properties of circles — equal chords are equidistant from the centre, the perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre, and tangents from an external point are equal in length.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A chord-and-tangent manipulator where dragging two tangents from an external point visually confirms their equal lengths, and dragging chords shows perpendicular bisectors converging at the centre.

## Strand 5: Mensuration

#### 5.1 Units of Measure
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Using metric units of mass, length, area, volume, and capacity, and converting between larger/smaller units, including area units (cm²↔m²) and volume/capacity units (m³↔litres).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A unit-conversion ladder tool where dragging a value up/down a metric-prefix scale live-recomputes its converted value.

#### 5.2 Area and Perimeter
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating the perimeter and area of rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, and trapeziums.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A shape-decomposition tool where a trapezium or parallelogram is split into simpler triangles/rectangles to derive its area formula visually.

#### 5.3 Circles, Arcs and Sectors
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended removes the "sector angle is a factor of 360°" restriction and adds major/minor sector distinction)
- **What's taught:** Calculating circumference and area of a circle, and arc length/sector area as fractions of the whole (restricted to angles that are factors of 360° at Core; any angle, including major sectors, at Extended).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A pie-slice tool (shared design with the Jordan grade 10 arcs/sectors lesson) with a draggable central angle live-computing arc length and sector area.

#### 5.4 Surface Area and Volume
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Calculating surface area and volume of a cuboid, prism, cylinder, sphere, pyramid, and cone (prism meaning any solid with uniform cross-section).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A 3D solid explorer where dragging dimension sliders (radius, height, etc.) live-recomputes and displays surface area and volume formulas alongside the resized solid.

#### 5.5 Compound Shapes and Parts of Shapes
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds frustums)
- **What's taught:** Calculating perimeters/areas of compound shapes and parts of shapes (e.g. half a circle), and surface areas/volumes of compound solids and parts of solids (e.g. half a sphere; frustums at Extended).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A shape-combination builder where students assemble compound figures from basic shapes/solids and the tool sums the component areas/volumes live, including a frustum "slice off the top of a cone" animation.

## Strand 6: Trigonometry

#### 6.1 Pythagoras' Theorem
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Knowing and applying Pythagoras' theorem to find unknown side lengths in right triangles.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable right-triangle where the three squares on each side visually demonstrate a²+b²=c² as areas.

#### 6.2 Right-Angled Triangles
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds shortest-distance-to-a-line and elevation/depression calculations explicitly)
- **What's taught:** Using sine, cosine, and tangent ratios to solve for unknown sides/angles in right triangles, combined with Pythagoras' theorem for two-dimensional problems; Extended explicitly adds that perpendicular distance to a line is the shortest distance, and angle of elevation/depression calculations.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable right triangle with live-updating trig ratios, plus an elevation/depression scenario simulator (as in the Jordan grade 9 trig-applications lesson).

#### 6.3 Exact Trigonometric Values
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Knowing exact sin/cos/tan values for the standard angles 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° without a calculator.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A unit-circle flashcard tool where clicking a standard angle reveals its exact trig values derived from 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangle ratios.

#### 6.4 Trigonometric Functions
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Sketching and interpreting y=sin x, y=cos x, y=tan x over 0°–360°, and solving trigonometric equations within that range.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A live sine/cosine/tangent grapher synced to a rotating unit-circle radius (as in the Jordan grade 10 trig-function lesson).

#### 6.5 Non-Right-Angled Triangles
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Using the sine rule and cosine rule for any triangle (including obtuse angles and the ambiguous SSA case) and the ½ab·sin C area formula.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A triangle solver (as in the Jordan grade 10 law-of-sines/cosines lessons) that auto-selects the correct rule based on which sides/angles are known and flags the ambiguous case.

#### 6.6 Pythagoras' Theorem and Trigonometry in 3D
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Solving 3D problems with Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry, including finding the angle between a line and a plane.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A rotatable 3D solid (e.g. cuboid or pyramid) where students isolate right-triangle cross-sections to solve for a line-plane angle, similar to the Jordan grade 9 3D-problems lesson.

## Strand 7: Transformations and Vectors

#### 7.1 Transformations
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended allows any line/centre/negative & fractional scale factors and combined transformations)
- **What's taught:** Recognizing, describing, and drawing reflection, rotation, enlargement, and translation; Core restricts reflection to vertical/horizontal lines, rotation to the origin/vertices/midpoints through 90° multiples, and positive/fractional scale factors only; Extended allows reflection in any line, rotation about any centre, negative and fractional scale factors, and combinations of transformations.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A drag-and-transform tool where students apply reflection/rotation/enlargement/translation to a shape via draggable handles, chaining multiple transformations at the Extended level.

#### 7.2 Vectors in Two Dimensions
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Describing a translation using column-vector, AB, or bold-letter notation, and adding, subtracting, and scalar-multiplying vectors.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A tip-to-tail vector adder (shared design with the Jordan grade 10 vector-addition lesson) with a scalar-multiplication slider stretching/reversing a vector live.

#### 7.3 Magnitude of a Vector
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Calculating a vector's magnitude as √(x²+y²) using modulus notation.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A draggable vector arrow with a live right-triangle overlay showing its components and Pythagorean magnitude calculation.

#### 7.4 Vector Geometry
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Representing vectors as directed line segments, using position vectors, expressing vectors as sums/differences of two coplanar vectors, and using vectors to prove parallelism, collinearity, or solve ratio/similarity problems.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A vector-geometry proof sandbox where combining two base vectors with draggable scalar multiples lets students visually construct and test claims like "these three points are collinear."

## Strand 8: Probability

#### 8.1 Introduction to Probability
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Extended adds formal P(A) notation)
- **What's taught:** Using the 0–1 probability scale, calculating single-event probability, and using the complement rule P(not A)=1−P(A); Extended adds formal notation P(A) and P(A′).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A probability-scale slider where dragging a marker between 0 and 1 shows example real-world events at that likelihood, plus a complement calculator.

#### 8.2 Relative and Expected Frequencies
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Understanding relative frequency as a probability estimate from experiments (e.g. spinner trials) and calculating expected frequencies for a population, including the concepts of fair, biased, and random.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A virtual spinner/dice simulator running many trials live, plotting relative frequency converging toward theoretical probability as trial count increases.

#### 8.3 Probability of Combined Events
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Core: with-replacement only; Extended: with or without replacement, plus P(A∩B)/P(A∪B) notation)
- **What's taught:** Calculating combined-event probabilities using sample space diagrams, two-set Venn diagrams, and tree diagrams; Core restricts combined events to with-replacement, Extended allows without-replacement and introduces set-intersection/union probability notation.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A tree-diagram builder (as in the Jordan grade 8/9 counting-outcomes lessons) with a toggle for with/without replacement that changes the branch probabilities after each draw.

#### 8.4 Conditional Probability
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Calculating conditional probability using Venn diagrams, tree diagrams, and tables (without requiring formal P(A|B) notation or formulas).
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A two-way table probability tool (as in the Jordan grade 10 dependent-events lesson) where selecting a row/column subset live-computes the conditional probability of the remaining category.

## Strand 9: Statistics

#### 9.1 Classifying Statistical Data
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Classifying and tabulating statistical data, e.g. using tally tables and two-way tables.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A drag-and-sort data classifier where raw data items are dragged into a tally table or two-way table, with live count totals.

#### 9.2 Interpreting Statistical Data
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Reading, interpreting, and drawing inferences from tables and statistical diagrams, comparing data sets using tables/graphs/statistical measures, and recognizing restrictions on conclusions drawn from given data.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A side-by-side dual-dataset comparison view highlighting differences in averages/spread as sliders adjust which measures are shown.

#### 9.3 Averages and Range / Measures of Spread
- **Tier:** Core & Extended (Core: mean/median/mode/range for ungrouped data; Extended adds quartiles, interquartile range, and estimating mean/modal class for grouped data)
- **What's taught:** Calculating mean, median, mode, and range for individual (ungrouped) data at Core; Extended adds quartiles and interquartile range, estimating the mean for grouped discrete/continuous data, and identifying the modal class from a grouped frequency distribution.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A live dot-plot/dataset editor (as in the Jordan grade 8/9 dispersion lessons) recomputing mean, median, mode, quartiles, and range as data points are dragged.

#### 9.4 Statistical Charts and Diagrams
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Drawing and interpreting bar charts (including composite/stacked and dual/side-by-side), pie charts, pictograms, stem-and-leaf diagrams (ordered, with a key), and simple frequency distributions.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A "same data, every chart type" toggle view (as in the Jordan grade 8 choosing-representation lesson) showing raw data rendered simultaneously in each chart format.

#### 9.5 Scatter Diagrams
- **Tier:** Core & Extended
- **What's taught:** Drawing/interpreting scatter diagrams, understanding positive/negative/zero correlation, and drawing by eye and using a line of best fit that roughly balances points on either side.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A scatter-plot best-fit-line dragger (shared design with the Jordan grade 10 scatter-plot lesson) with live feedback on fit quality.

#### 9.6 Cumulative Frequency Diagrams
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Drawing and interpreting cumulative frequency tables and diagrams (smooth curve through plotted points), and estimating median, percentiles, quartiles, and interquartile range from the curve.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A grouped-data-to-ogive builder (as in the Jordan grade 10 cumulative frequency lesson) with draggable guide lines reading off quartile/percentile values.

#### 9.7 Histograms
- **Tier:** Extended only
- **What's taught:** Drawing and interpreting histograms with unequal class widths, using frequency density (frequency ÷ class width) as the bar-height measure on the vertical axis.
- **Visualization/interaction potential:** A histogram builder (shared design with the Jordan grade 9 frequency-histogram lesson) where changing class-interval widths auto-recalculates frequency density and bar heights.
