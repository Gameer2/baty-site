What to build

Both systems converge on the same advanced destinations by grade 10 (functions, trigonometry, analytic geometry, statistics) but reach them on different schedules and with different emphasis. The leverage is in the shared middle — build that once, then bolt on the system-specific ends.

Shared core — build once

Appears in both systems across most grades. One engine module serves both curricula.

  • Arithmetic & number sense (G4–6): place value, four operations, divisibility
  • Fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio & proportion (G4–8)
  • Intro algebra & linear equations (G7–8)
  • Geometry & measurement: area, volume, angles, polygons (all grades)
  • Statistics basics: mean/median/mode/range & charts (G6–9)
  • Probability basics: 0–1 scale, equally likely, combined events (G7–9)
  • Pythagoras (G8–9), basic trig ratios (G9–10)
  • Simultaneous & quadratic equations, analytic geometry of lines (G9–10)
Highest coverage per module. Start here — roughly 70% of both curricula.

Jordan-only extras

Topics the national curriculum carries that Cambridge 0580 doesn't, or covers much later.

  • Sets & set operations (G7)
  • Commercial math: insurance, stocks (G8) — interest is shared
  • 3D space geometry: planes, projections (G10)
  • Polynomial division, synthetic division, remainder theorem (G10)
  • Compound/multiple/half-angle trig identities & equations (G10)
  • Periodic functions, radians, arc length (G10)
Jordan pushes deeper & earlier on trig identities and 3D space geometry.

Cambridge-only extras

Topics the IGCSE Extended tier carries that Jordan's G4–10 doesn't reach.

  • Surds (simplify, rationalise), exact trig values (30/45/60°)
  • Sine & cosine rules, ½ab sin C, 3D trig
  • Vectors & vector geometry (magnitude, position, parallel proofs)
  • Conditional probability, with/without replacement, 3-set Venn
  • Cumulative frequency, histograms (frequency density), quartiles/IQR
  • Differentiation intro (gradients, stationary points)
  • Thinking & Working Mathematically — a process skill, not a topic
Cambridge goes deeper on statistics/probability tooling and vectors.

Suggested build order

One way to sequence the engine work, by leverage.

  • 1 · Number & arithmetic engine (G4–6 shared)
  • 2 · Fractions/decimals/%/ratio engine (G4–8 shared)
  • 3 · Algebra & equations engine (G7–10 shared)
  • 4 · Geometry & measurement engine (all grades shared)
  • 5 · Statistics & probability engine (G6–10 shared)
  • 6 · Functions/trig/analytic engine (G8–10 shared)
  • 7 · Jordan extension pack (sets, 3D space, poly division, trig identities)
  • 8 · Cambridge Extended pack (surds, sine/cosine rules, vectors, conditional prob, cumulative freq)
Steps 1–6 cover both systems; 7 & 8 are the system-specific caps.
