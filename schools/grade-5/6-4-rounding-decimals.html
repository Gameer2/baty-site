Rounding Decimals

تقريب الكسور العشرية

Objective — I round a decimal to the nearest whole number, tenth, or hundredth by finding which rounding target it's closer to.

snaps to whichever target is closer

What this teaches: Rounding means finding which of two nearby "round" targets a value sits closer to, then snapping to it — the number line makes the distance to each target, and therefore the correct direction to round, directly visible.
