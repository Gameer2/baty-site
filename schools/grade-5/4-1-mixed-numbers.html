Mixed Numbers

الأعداد الكسرية

Objective — I represent a value as an improper fraction and as a mixed number, and see that they name the same amount.

two names, one amount

What this teaches: A mixed number and its equivalent improper fraction name the exact same point on the number line — the pies show why: whole circles plus one partial circle is the same total area as one big stack of eighths.
