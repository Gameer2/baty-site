The Statistical Question

السؤال الإحصائي

Objective — I recognize a statistical question as one that anticipates variability in the answers it collects.

does the answer vary from person to person?
answers varied — that's a statistical question

What this teaches: A statistical question expects a range of different answers across the people asked; a question with one fixed correct answer for everyone is not statistical, even if it involves numbers.
