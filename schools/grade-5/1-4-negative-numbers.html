Reading the number line

What this teaches: Introduces negative numbers as representing values below a zero reference point (sea level, ground floor, temperature below freezing), using a vertical number-line model. Students read a position as a signed number from a diagram, and place given numbers on a number line, distinguishing negative from positive by their side of zero.
