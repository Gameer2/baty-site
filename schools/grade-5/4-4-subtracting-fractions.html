Subtracting Fractions

طرح الكسور

Objective — I subtract two fractions with unlike denominators by resizing both to a shared denominator, then taking pieces away.

resize to match, then take pieces away

What this teaches: Subtracting fractions is a take-away: once both fractions are resized to the same denominator, the difference is just how many equal-size pieces are left after removing the subtrahend's share.
