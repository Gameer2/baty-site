Least Common Multiple

المضاعف المشترك الأصغر

Objective — I find the least common multiple of two whole numbers.

when do the marked teeth line up again?

What this teaches: Two gears with different tooth counts only bring their marked teeth back together after each has completed a whole number of turns — exactly at their least common multiple, the same value you land on skip-counting both numbers.
