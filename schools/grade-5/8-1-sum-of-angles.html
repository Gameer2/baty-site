Sum of Angles on a Line and Around a Point

مجموع الزوايا على خط وحول نقطة

Objective — I use the fact that angles on a line sum to 180° and angles around a point sum to 360° to find an unknown angle.

drag a ray, watch the rest compensate

What this teaches: Angles that sit along a straight line always total 180°, and angles that surround a single point always total 360° — dragging one angle bigger always shrinks the others by exactly the same amount, keeping the fixed sum.
