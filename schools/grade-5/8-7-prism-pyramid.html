Prism and Pyramid

المنشور والهرم

Objective — I identify the faces, edges, and vertices of a prism and a pyramid by folding and unfolding their nets.

unfold fully to count F, E, and V

What this teaches: A 3D solid's flat net is made of the exact same faces, just unfolded along their shared edges — counting faces, edges, and vertices is far easier on the flattened net than on the folded solid, and refolding confirms nothing was added or lost.
