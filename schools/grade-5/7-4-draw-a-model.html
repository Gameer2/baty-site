Problem-Solving Strategy: Draw a Model

استراتيجية حل المسألة: ارسم نموذجًا

Objective — I solve real-life problems by building a tape diagram that turns the relationship between two quantities into an equation.

the equation falls out of the diagram

What this teaches: Drawing a tape diagram — one bar per quantity, sized to show how they relate — turns a word problem's relationship into a visual you can read an equation straight off of, without guessing which operation to use.
