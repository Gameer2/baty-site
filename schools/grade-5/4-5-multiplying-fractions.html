Multiplying Fractions

ضرب الكسور

Objective — I multiply two fractions using an area model, where the overlap of shaded rows and columns is the product.

rows meet columns in the overlap

What this teaches: Multiplying two fractions is an area model: shade a fraction of the rows for one factor and a fraction of the columns for the other — the doubly-shaded overlap region is exactly the product, with denominator equal to the total number of cells.
