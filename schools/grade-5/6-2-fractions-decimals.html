Converting Between Fractions and Decimals

التحويل بين الكسور والكسور العشرية

Objective — I convert a fraction to a decimal by dividing, and a decimal to a fraction over a power of ten.

the same value, two representations

What this teaches: A fraction converts to a decimal by long division of its numerator by its denominator; a decimal converts to a fraction by reading its place value as a power-of-ten denominator, then simplifying if the numerator and denominator share a common factor.
