Objective — I compare the theoretical chance of an outcome to what repeated random draws actually produce.

bigger slice, more likely — draws should match

What this teaches: An outcome's slice size sets its theoretical chance of occurring — repeated random draws won't match that chance exactly every time, but over enough spins the empirical counts drift toward the same proportions the slices predict.
