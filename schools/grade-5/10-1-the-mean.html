The Mean

الوسط الحسابي

Objective — I find the mean of a data set, and see it as the balance point of the values.

sum of values ÷ count of values

What this teaches: The mean is the value that "balances" a data set — literally the point where the total distance of values above it equals the total distance of values below it, which is exactly why one extreme outlier can pull the mean noticeably toward itself.
