Greatest Common Factor

القاسم المشترك الأكبر

Objective — I find the greatest common factor of two whole numbers by sorting their factors into a Venn diagram.

tap each factor to sort it

What this teaches: Every factor of a number sorts into exactly one region of the Venn diagram; the numbers shared by both circles are common factors, and the largest of those is the greatest common factor.
