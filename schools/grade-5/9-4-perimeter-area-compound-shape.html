Perimeter and Area of a Compound Shape

محيط ومساحة شكل مركّب

Objective — I find the area of an L-shaped compound figure by decomposing it into rectangles, or by completing and subtracting.

two methods, same answer

What this teaches: An L-shaped compound figure's area can be found two ways: splitting it into two rectangles and adding their areas, or completing it into one big rectangle and subtracting the missing notch — both methods land on the exact same total.
