Objective — I compare two fractions by rewriting them on a shared grid of equal-size pieces.

same-size pieces make comparison visible

What this teaches: Fractions can only be compared directly once they're cut into pieces of the same size — rewriting both fractions over a common denominator makes "which is bigger" a simple visual read, not a guess.
