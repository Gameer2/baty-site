Polygons

Objective — I identify a polygon as a closed, straight-sided shape with no crossing sides.

closed, straight sides, no crossings

What this teaches: A polygon must be closed (no gaps), made only of straight sides (no curves), and have sides that don't cross each other — a shape failing any one of those three tests isn't a polygon.
