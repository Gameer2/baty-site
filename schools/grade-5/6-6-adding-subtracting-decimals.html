Adding and Subtracting Decimals

جمع وطرح الكسور العشرية

Objective — I add or subtract decimals by aligning their decimal points, padding missing places with zero.

align the points, pad with zero, carry as usual

What this teaches: Adding or subtracting decimals starts with lining up the decimal points so matching place values sit in the same column — any place one number is "missing" gets a zero pad — then the same carry/borrow process from whole-number addition runs unchanged, with the decimal point simply dropped straight down into the answer.
