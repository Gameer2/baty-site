The Median and Mode

الوسيط والمنوال

Objective — I find the median by crossing off pairs from both ends, and the mode by finding the most frequent value.

the middle value, and the most common one

What this teaches: Sorted data's median is found by crossing off the smallest and largest values in pairs until only the middle one (or two, averaged) remains; the mode is simply whichever value shows up most often, read straight off a frequency count.
