Addition and Subtraction Equations

معادلات الجمع والطرح

Objective — I solve one-step addition and subtraction equations by keeping both sides of the equation balanced.

remove the same amount from both sides

What this teaches: An equation is a balance: whatever you remove (or add) from one side, you must remove (or add) from the other side too, or the balance breaks. Removing the constant from the variable's side isolates x, and substituting the result back in checks the answer.
