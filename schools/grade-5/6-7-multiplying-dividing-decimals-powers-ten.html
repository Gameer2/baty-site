Multiplying and Dividing Decimals by 10, 100, 1000

الضرب والقسمة على ١٠، ١٠٠، ١٠٠٠

Objective — I multiply or divide a decimal by 10, 100, or 1000 by moving its decimal point.

the point jumps, the digits don't move

What this teaches: Multiplying or dividing a decimal by a power of ten never changes its digits — only the decimal point's position, which jumps right for multiplication and left for division, one place per zero in the power of ten.
