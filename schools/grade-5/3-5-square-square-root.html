Square of a Number and Square Root

مربّع العدد والجذر التربيعي

Objective — I find the square of a whole number, and the square root of a perfect square, using a tile-grid model.

count the tiles in the square
6

What this teaches: A number's square is literally the number of unit tiles that fill an n-by-n square; the square root of a perfect-square area is the side length of that same square — the two operations undo each other.
