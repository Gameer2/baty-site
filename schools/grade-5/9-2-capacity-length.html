Units of Capacity and Length

وحدات السعة والطول

Objective — I convert between metric units of length or capacity by climbing a unit ladder.

each rung shifts the decimal point

What this teaches: Metric units form a ladder, each rung a power of ten apart — climbing up (bigger unit) or down (smaller unit) shifts the decimal point by exactly that many places, the same rule from multiplying and dividing decimals by powers of ten.
