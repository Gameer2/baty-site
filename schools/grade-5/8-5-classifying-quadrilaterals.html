Objective — I classify a quadrilateral by its properties, seeing how trapezoid, parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, and square nest inside each other.

drag any vertex

What this teaches: Quadrilateral categories nest: every parallelogram is a trapezoid, every rectangle and rhombus is a parallelogram, and a square is both a rectangle and a rhombus at once — dragging the shape toward a special case lights up every category it now satisfies, not just one.
