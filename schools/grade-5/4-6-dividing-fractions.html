Dividing Fractions

قسمة الكسور

Objective — I divide a fraction by a fraction by measuring how many copies of the divisor fit into the dividend.

how many divisor-copies fit?

What this teaches: Dividing by a fraction means asking "how many copies of that fraction fit inside the dividend?" — measuring it out directly gives the same answer as the invert-and-multiply shortcut, which is really just a faster way to count copies.
