Time

الزمن

Objective — I find elapsed time and convert it into compound days, hours, and minutes.

start time + elapsed = end time

What this teaches: A raw elapsed-minutes count converts into compound form (days, hours, minutes) the same way place value works: divide by 1440 for days, divide the remainder by 60 for hours, and whatever's left is minutes — then adding that elapsed time to a start time gives the end time.
