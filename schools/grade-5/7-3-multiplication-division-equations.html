Multiplication and Division Equations

Objective — I solve one-step multiplication and division equations by splitting or merging equal groups on both sides.

equal groups on both sides reveal x

What this teaches: Solving 3x = 12 means splitting both sides into 3 equal groups — one group is x. Solving x ÷ 4 = 5 means the reverse: x was already split into 4 groups of 5, so merging those groups back together reveals x.
