Units of Mass

وحدات الكتلة

Objective — I convert between grams and kilograms, and express a mass in compound form.

1000 g = 1 kg

What this teaches: 1000 grams make one kilogram, so converting between them is a ×1000 or ÷1000 jump; a mass can also be written in compound form — a whole number of kilograms plus the leftover grams — which is often how mass is actually read off a real scale.
