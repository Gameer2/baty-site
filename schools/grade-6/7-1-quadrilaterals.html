Objective — I use the fact that a quadrilateral's angles sum to 360° to find an unknown angle.

What this teaches: Building on grade 5's quadrilateral classification, students now use the interior-angle-sum property (360°) to solve for a missing angle given the other three, applied to a real four-legged table/drafting-board shape.
