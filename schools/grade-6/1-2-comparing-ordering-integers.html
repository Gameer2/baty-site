Comparing & Ordering Integers

مقارنة الأعداد الصحيحة وترتيبها

Objective — I compare integers and order them.

A compared to B
−3 m
−8 m
0 — sea level
A: −3 m
B: −8 m

What this teaches: On a number line, values increase rightward/upward and decrease leftward/downward, so positive integers are always greater than negative ones; among negatives, the one closer to zero is greater. Applied to comparing burial/hole depths and similar below-ground measurements.
