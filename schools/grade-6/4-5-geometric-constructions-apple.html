Construction steps

What this teaches: Constructions use only an unmarked straightedge and compass — no ruler measurement, no protractor. A perpendicular bisector and an angle bisector are both built from equal-radius arcs and their intersections: the crossing points are equidistant from the two centers by construction, not by measuring, which is exactly why the resulting line is provably correct.
