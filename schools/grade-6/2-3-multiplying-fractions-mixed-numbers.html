Multiplying Fractions & Mixed Numbers

ضرب الكسور والأعداد الكسرية

Objective — I find the product of fractions and mixed numbers in simplest form.

four partial products, one per whole/fraction pairing
×
whole × whole
A's whole × B's fraction
A's fraction × B's whole
fraction × fraction

What this teaches: Multiply numerators and denominators directly for fractions; convert mixed numbers to improper fractions first, then multiply and simplify (canceling common factors before multiplying where possible). Applied to comparing mountain-peak heights as a fraction of another peak's height.
