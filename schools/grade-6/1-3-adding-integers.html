What this teaches: Same-sign addition sums the absolute values and keeps the shared sign; different-sign addition subtracts the smaller absolute value from the larger and takes the sign of the larger-magnitude number. A number plus its additive inverse (opposite) equals zero. Every result is verified by tracing hops on a number line. Real contexts: net position after running forward/backward, diving then ascending toward the surface.
