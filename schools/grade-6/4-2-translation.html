Translation in the Coordinate Plane

الانسحاب في المستوى الإحداثي

Objective — I draw a translation in the coordinate plane.

(x, y) → (x + a, y + b)
+3
−2

What this teaches: A translation slides every point of a shape the same horizontal and vertical amount without rotating, producing a congruent image. Students describe a given translation from a diagram, and execute one by moving each vertex a stated number of units right/left and up/down, both by hand and via coordinates ((x,y)→(x+a, y+b)).
