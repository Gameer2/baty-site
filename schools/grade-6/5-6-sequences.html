What this teaches: A sequence is an ordered list of numbers following a rule linking consecutive terms; students find that rule (constant addition/subtraction, shown via a growing geometric dot/shape pattern) and extend the sequence to find further terms — without yet deriving a general algebraic nth-term formula (that comes in grade 7).
