Integers & absolute value

What this teaches: Integers extend whole numbers below zero (floors below ground, temperatures below freezing). Two numbers are opposites if equidistant from zero on opposite sides (zero is its own opposite, neither positive nor negative). Absolute value is the distance from zero, always non-negative, applied to real vertical-distance problems (bird above vs. fish below sea level, summed via |a|+|b|).
