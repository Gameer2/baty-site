Powers & Exponents

القوى والأسس

Objective — I use powers and exponents to write multiplication expressions.

stack copies of the base, one per exponent
4

What this teaches: Repeated multiplication of a number by itself is written compactly with an exponent (base^exponent = "power"); students convert between exponential form and standard (expanded product) form, read powers aloud ("seven squared," "seven cubed"), and note that a number with no visible exponent is implicitly to the power 1. Applies to variables too (j×j×j×j = j⁴).
