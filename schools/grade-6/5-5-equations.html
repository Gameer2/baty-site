Objective — I verify whether a given value solves an equation; I solve equations.

What this teaches: An equation's solution is a variable value that makes both sides equal, verified by substitution. Building on a concept activity for two-operation equations, students solve equations requiring more than one step, isolating the variable via inverse operations.
