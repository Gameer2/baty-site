Circle Sectors (Pie Charts)

القطاعات الدائرية

Objective — I read, interpret, and solve problems involving data represented by circle sectors (pie charts).

sector angle = share of the whole × 360°

What this teaches: Each sector's size represents a category's share of the total (proportion), making pie charts best for comparing a category against the whole — as opposed to bar graphs, better for comparing categories against each other. Students read fractions/percentages directly off sector sizes.
