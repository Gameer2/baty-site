What this teaches: For unlike fractions, find the LCM of both denominators, rewrite each fraction with that common denominator (scaling numerator and denominator together), then add/subtract numerators and simplify — extending beyond grade 5's "one denominator is a multiple of the other" restriction to any two denominators. Applied to comparing planetary diameters as fractions of Earth's, and measuring-cup recipe adjustments.
