Probabilities

Objective — I describe the probability of an event using a numerical value on the probability scale.

0 = impossible, 1 = certain
0.5

What this teaches: Probability is a number from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain) placed on a probability scale, with labeled regions (impossible, unlikely, equally likely, likely, certain). Students judge and place events on this qualitative-to-quantitative scale, extending the informal "equal/unequal chance" idea from grade 5 toward numeric probability.
