Square Root & Cube Root

Objective — I find square roots and cube roots of numbers.

count the tiles
4

What this teaches: The square root of a perfect square is the whole number that, multiplied by itself, gives it (√9=3 since 3×3=9); the cube root of a perfect cube is the number multiplied by itself three times to give it (∛8=2 since 2×2×2=8). Applied to finding a square game board's side length from its area.
