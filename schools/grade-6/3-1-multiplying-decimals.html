What this teaches: Multiply as if both numbers were whole numbers, then place the decimal point so the result has as many decimal places as the two factors combined (padding with a leading zero if needed). A second method factors a multiple of 10/100/1000 out of one factor first. Also covers evaluating algebraic expressions (3x) by substituting a decimal for x. Applied to tree-growth-rate and cycling-speed problems.
