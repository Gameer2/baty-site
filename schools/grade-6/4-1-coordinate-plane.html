The Coordinate Plane

المستوى الإحداثي

Objective — I recognize the coordinate plane, and locate points on all four quadrants.

(x, y) and which quadrant it falls in

What this teaches: Extends grade 5's first-quadrant-only plane to all four quadrants (numbered counterclockwise from top-right), with negative coordinates now meaningful. Students read a point's coordinates and identify its quadrant (or axis, if a coordinate is zero) from a diagram, and plot given ordered pairs by moving from the origin, applied to a zoo map and city-location contexts.
