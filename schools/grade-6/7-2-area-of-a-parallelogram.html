Area of a Parallelogram

مساحة متوازي الأضلاع

Objective — I find the area of a parallelogram.

area = base × height, however it's slanted

What this teaches: Area = base × height, where height is the perpendicular distance between the base and its opposite side (not the slanted side length) — applied to a vegetable-cutting board shaped as a parallelogram.
