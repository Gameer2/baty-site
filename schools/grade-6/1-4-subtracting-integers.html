What this teaches: Subtraction is redefined as "add the opposite" (a−b = a+(−b)), covering subtracting a positive (moves left/down the amount) and subtracting a negative (moves right/up, i.e. subtracting a negative increases the value). Preceded by a counter-tile concept activity linking removal-based subtraction to the add-the-opposite rule. Applied to temperature differences between Earth and Mars, crane-lowering distances, and archaeological depth problems.
