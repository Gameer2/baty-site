Sampling Distributions & the CLT

Repeatedly draw samples of size n from a chosen population and watch the distribution of sample means tighten toward a bell curve centered on the population mean — however skewed the population itself is. That's the Central Limit Theorem, and the reason the site's one-sample t-test is valid at all.
