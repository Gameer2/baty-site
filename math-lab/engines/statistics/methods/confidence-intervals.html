Confidence Intervals

The counterpart to the t-test: instead of "is the mean equal to this value?", ask "what range plausibly contains the true mean, proportion, or variance?" Builds an interval for a mean, proportion, or variance — with the critical value solved by bisection on the reference distribution, not looked up from a table.
