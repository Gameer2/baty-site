One-Way ANOVA (F-Test)

Compares the means of three or more groups at once — the standard next step after the two-sample t-test, instead of running many pairwise tests. The total variability splits into between-group (explained by group membership) and within-group (residual); the F statistic is their ratio, and the p-value comes from the exact F-distribution CDF (regularized incomplete beta), not a table lookup.

Example 3 groups, 5 values each. At least 2 groups; every group non-empty.
