Linear Algebra

Hand It Your Matrix

Paste a matrix — and a right-hand side, if you're solving a system. The engine reads its structure (symmetric? positive-definite? diagonally dominant?), picks the matching method the way a numerical analyst actually would, and shows the full working: the row reduction, the factorization, or the iteration, whichever applies. No special structure? It falls back to the general-purpose route — Gaussian elimination, or shifted QR for eigenvalues — clearly labeled as the fallback, not a failure.
