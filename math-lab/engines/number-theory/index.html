Integers, Exactly

No Floating Point. No Approximation.

Every other engine on the Lab fights numeric drift — a symbolic result quietly wrong by 1e-9, a rational approximation of π. This one doesn't have that problem. gcd(48, 180) = 12 is exactly 12, forever, computed with BigInt from the ground up. Divisibility, primes, congruences and cryptography — worked step by step, the way Euclid, Fermat and Gauss actually did it.
