Jacobi Symbol

The Jacobi symbol (a/n) generalises the Legendre symbol to any odd positive n, defined as the product of Legendre symbols over the prime factors of n. It's computed by the same reciprocity-based algorithm — *without factoring n* — which makes it fast even for enormous n. One warning the page makes plain: when n is composite, (a/n) = +1 does NOT mean a is a square mod n.

Input
Try (2/15): the Jacobi symbol is +1, yet 2 is NOT a square mod 15 — the trap that comes from n being composite.
