Discrete Logarithm

Given g, h, n, find x with g^x ≡ h (mod n) — the discrete log. Easy to state, believed hard to solve at scale (the security of Diffie-Hellman rests on exactly that). Baby-step giant-step solves it in ~√n steps by precomputing a table of g^j and then stepping down in giant strides of g^(−m). The work is visible here, and so is the "not found" answer when h is outside the subgroup g generates.

Input — solve g^x ≡ h (mod n)
Try 3^x ≡ 4 (mod 7) → x = 4. With a primitive root g, every unit is reachable; with a non-generator, many h have no solution.
