Law of Quadratic Reciprocity

For distinct odd primes p, q, the Legendre symbols flip together or apart in a precise way: (p/q)(q/p) = (−1)^(((p−1)(q−1))/4). The deepest theorem in this course. This engine cannot prove it — it's a theorem, not an algorithm — but it can *demonstrate* it on any pair: compute each symbol by Euler's criterion and watch the product match the sign law exactly.

Input — two odd primes p, q
Both must be odd primes. The page computes (p/q), (q/p) by Euler's criterion and checks them against the reciprocity sign (−1)^(((p−1)(q−1))/4).
