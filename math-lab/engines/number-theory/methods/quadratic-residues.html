Quadratic Residues & the Legendre Symbol

A quadratic residue mod p is a square: some x² lands on it. Exactly half the non-zero residues mod an odd prime are squares. The Legendre symbol (a/p) encodes the answer as +1 (square), −1 (non-square), 0 (p | a), and Euler's criterion computes it as a^((p−1)/2) mod p — no square root required.

Input
Enter an odd prime p and any a. The page lists every QR mod p, checks a with the Legendre symbol, and shows the modular square root when one exists (Tonelli–Shanks).
Output

Enter an odd prime and a — the QR list, the Legendre symbol, the Euler-criterion check, and any square root will appear here.
