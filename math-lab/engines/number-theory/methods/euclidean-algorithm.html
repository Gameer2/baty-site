Divisibility & the Euclidean Algorithm

Euclidean Algorithm

Repeatedly applies the Division Algorithm — a = bq + r — replacing (a, b) with (b, r) until the remainder hits zero. Whatever's left is gcd(a, b), exactly, every time — no floating point, no approximation.

Input
Any whole numbers — arbitrarily large. Computed with exact integer (BigInt) arithmetic, never floating point.
Output

Enter two integers and compute — every division step, the shrinking-remainder visual, and the final gcd will appear here.
