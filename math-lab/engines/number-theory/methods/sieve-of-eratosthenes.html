Sieve of Eratosthenes

The oldest algorithm that finds every prime up to n in one pass. Start at 2; mark it prime, strike every multiple; move to the next unmarked number — that's prime too — and strike its multiples. What's left standing is exactly the primes below n. The animation is the lesson: composites die in coloured waves, one prime at a time.

Input
2 through n. Keep it small (≤ 400) — the *animation* is the point, and each multiple is struck one cell at a time.
Output

Choose a limit and sieve — the grid will populate and the step slider will walk through each prime's strike wave.
