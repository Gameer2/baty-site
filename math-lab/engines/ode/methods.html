PDE 7 / 9

Laplace & Poisson Equations

u_xx + u_yy = 0 (Laplace) or = f(x,y) (Poisson) on a rectangle with Dirichlet data. Laplace is solved two independent ways — the sinh-series from separation of variables and numeric relaxation on the 5-point stencil — and shown only once they agree; Poisson is solved by relaxation and verified by substituting back into the discrete equation.

PDE 8 / 9

Fourier Series

Expand a function as a sum of sines and cosines — the full series on [-L, L], or the half-range sine (odd) and cosine (even) series on [0, L] that the heat and wave equations need. Coefficients by Simpson's rule, with exact π-symbolic forms where the integral is elementary, and the Gibbs phenomenon visible at a jump.
