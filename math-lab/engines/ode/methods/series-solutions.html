Series Solutions

Homogeneous p(x)y'' + q(x)y' + r(x)y = 0 with variable coefficients, expanded as a power series around a point — ordinary, where q/p and r/p are both finite (a plain Taylor series, always safe), or regular singular, where the indicial equation's roots are computed first. If those roots are equal or differ by an integer, the second solution may need a logarithmic term SymPy's series hint is known to omit without warning — verified directly against Bessel's equation of both orders 0 and 1 — so this page refuses those cases rather than risk showing an incomplete general solution.
