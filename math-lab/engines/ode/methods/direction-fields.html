Direction Field & Euler vs RK4

The numeric machinery underneath every symbolic solver on this engine. Steer dy/dx = f(x, y) forward from an initial point with Euler's method and RK4 side by side, overlaid on the slope field. When f happens to be linear in x and y, the exact closed form is detected and plotted too — so the error of each method is right on the screen. This is the labelled fallback the ODE Solver hands off to when no symbolic method matches.
