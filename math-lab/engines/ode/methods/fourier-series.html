Partial Differential Equations

Fourier Series

Expand a function as a sum of sines and cosines — the prerequisite for every separation-of-variables PDE on this engine. The full series on [-L, L] uses both aₙ and bₙ; the half-range sine series (odd extension) and half-range cosine series (even extension) on [0, L] match the boundary conditions the heat and wave equations impose. Coefficients are computed by Simpson's rule for every n, and — where the integral is elementary — also exactly with π kept symbolic, so the 1/n-type pattern shows across rows. The partial sum converging against the target reveals the Gibbs phenomenon: the persistent overshoot at a jump that never goes away.
