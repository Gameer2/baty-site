Functions You Can't Graph.

A complex function needs two dimensions in and two out — a 4-dimensional graph that no curve or surface can show. That's not a presentation problem, it's the problem of the subject. This engine solves it: domain colouring paints the whole plane, conformal maps show angles surviving a transformation, and the residue theorem turns impossible real integrals into a walk around a pole. Built on the same verified CAS core as the Calculus engine.
