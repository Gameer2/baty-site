Integration & Residues 8 / 12

Cauchy's Integral Formula

The value of an analytic function at a point — and every derivative there — is fixed by one loop around it: f(n)(z₀) = n!/(2πi)·∮ f(z)/(z−z₀)n+1 dz. Give f(z), z₀ and a radius and the page evaluates it on a circle about z₀, then verifies the answer on a second, independent circle (and, for n=0, against a direct f(z₀)) — no symbolic backend, just two independent numeric integrations that must agree.

Laurent Series & Singularity Classification

Integration & Residues 10 / 12

Real Integrals by Residues

Integrals over the whole real line — or the half-line — that no elementary antiderivative touches, done by lifting R(x) to the plane and closing the contour with a large upper semicircle: ∫ R(x)dx = 2πi·ΣRes over the poles above the real axis. Handles the bread-and-butter case (non-oscillatory, fast-decaying, no real-axis poles) and refuses the rest. The residue answer is re-checked by a direct numeric integration (tangent substitution) before it's shown.

Integration & Residues 11 / 12

Argument Principle & Rouché's Theorem

Count zeros and poles from a single loop. The argument principle: (1/2πi)∮ f′/f dz = N − P, the winding of f(γ) about the origin. Rouché: if |f−g| < |f| on γ, then f and g have the same number of zeros inside. Both are checked two independent ways — winding number against the logarithmic-derivative integral — and the image curve f(γ) is drawn in the w-plane so the winding is visible.

Conformal & Möbius Mappings

The basic conformal map w = (az+b)/(cz+d) — circles-and-lines to circles-and-lines, angles preserved. Give the four coefficients and this page works out the fixed points, the pole sent to ∞, the multiplier, and the type (parabolic / elliptic / hyperbolic / loxodromic), then draws an orthogonal grid in the z-plane next to its image in the w-plane. Pure arithmetic on the existing Complex module — no symbolic backend — with every fixed point re-checked by substitution.
