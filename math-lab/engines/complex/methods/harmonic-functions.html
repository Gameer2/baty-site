The Plane & Analyticity

Harmonic Functions & Conjugates

Every analytic f = u + iv forces both u and v to satisfy Laplace's equation, u_xx + u_yy = 0 — the real and imaginary parts of an analytic function are harmonic. The converse is the useful direction: given any harmonic u(x, y), the Cauchy-Riemann equations pin down its conjugate v up to a constant. This page recovers it exactly, by symbolic integration, and plots the level curves of u and v — which, by Cauchy-Riemann, always cross at right angles. The same equation governs steady-state heat and electrostatic potential — the ODE/PDE Engine doesn't have a dedicated Laplace's-equation page yet, but this is exactly the equation it would solve.
