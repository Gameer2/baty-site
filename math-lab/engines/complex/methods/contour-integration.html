Contour Integration & Residues

Give a function f(z) and a closed circular contour γ (center + radius), and the residue theorem does the rest: ∮γ f(z)dz = 2πi · Σ Res, summed over every singularity of f that lies inside γ. SymPy finds the singularities and their residues; the answer is never shown on that alone — it's independently checked by numerically walking the actual contour and comparing. This is the same residue-theorem machinery the ODE Engine's Laplace-transform inversion will reuse.
