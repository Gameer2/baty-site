Conformal & Möbius Mappings

A Möbius (bilinear) transformation w = (az+b)/(cz+d) is the basic building block of conformal mapping — it carries circles and lines to circles and lines and preserves angles. Give the four complex coefficients and this page works out, in closed form, the fixed points, the finite pole (the point sent to ∞), the multiplier, and the geometric type — parabolic, elliptic, hyperbolic, or loxodromic — then draws an orthogonal grid in the z-plane against its image in the w-plane so you can watch the conformality directly. Pure arithmetic on the existing Complex module: no symbolic backend, no lazy-load. Every fixed point is re-checked by substitution before it's shown.
