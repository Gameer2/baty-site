Integration & Residues

Real Integrals by Residues

Integrals over the whole real line — or the half-line — that no elementary antiderivative touches, done by lifting the real integral to the complex plane and closing the contour with a large semicircle in the upper half-plane: ∫ R(x)dx = 2πi·Σ Res over the poles above the real axis. This page handles the bread-and-butter case — a non-oscillatory rational R(x) that decays fast enough and has no real-axis poles — and refuses the rest (real-axis poles need an indented contour; oscillatory integrands need Jordan's lemma) rather than guess. The residue answer is never shown on SymPy's say-so alone: it is independently re-checked by a direct numeric integration (tangent substitution, so Simpson converges fast) before it appears.
