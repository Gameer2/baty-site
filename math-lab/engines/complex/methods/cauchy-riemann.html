The Plane & Analyticity

Analyticity & the Cauchy-Riemann Equations

Split f(z) = u(x,y) + i·v(x,y) and the question "is f analytic?" becomes two exact partial-derivative equations: u_x = v_y and u_y = -v_x. Both u and v are computed symbolically — not approximated — by walking f(z)'s expression tree and combining the complex-arithmetic identities directly, which sidesteps a real bug found in nerdamer's built-in real/imaginary-part extraction (see the build notes). The partials are then checked exactly at your point and sampled across the whole plane, which is what tells "analytic everywhere" apart from the textbook trap of a function whose equations happen to hold at one isolated point and nowhere else.
