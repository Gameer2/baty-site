Laurent Series & Singularity Classification

Give a function f(z) and a point z₀, and this page says exactly what kind of singularity lives there — removable, a pole of a specific order, an essential singularity, or a regular point — then writes the Laurent series about it. The verdict comes from the limit definitions (the honest test), not from eyeballing a series: SymPy's series expansion leaves an essential singularity like e^(1/z) unexpanded and refuses sin(1/z) outright, so a principal-part parse would misclassify both. Same answer-only discipline as the contour page: a verdict and a series, never a step-by-step derivation.
