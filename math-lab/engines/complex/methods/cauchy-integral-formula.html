Cauchy's Integral Formula

The crown jewel of complex analysis: the value of an analytic function at a point — and every one of its derivatives there — is determined entirely by a single loop around it. Give f(z), a point z₀ and a radius r, and this page evaluates f(n)(z₀) = n!/(2πi) · ∮|z−z₀|=r f(z)/(z−z₀)n+1 dz. The contour is a circle centred at z₀, the standard CIF setup. No symbolic backend is needed — but the answer is still never shown on one method's say-so: it is computed on two independent circles (radii r and r/2) and, for n = 0, also checked against a direct evaluation of f(z₀). The formula's answer is contour-independent for an analytic f, so two independent numeric integrations agreeing is the verification.
