Complex Functions & Domain Colouring

f: ℂ → ℂ needs two dimensions in and two out — a graph no curve or surface can show. Domain colouring paints the input plane instead: hue = arg f(z), brightness = |f(z)|. A zero darkens to black and the hue wheel winds around it once per order; a pole brightens to white the same way. Click anywhere on the plane to read off z and f(z) exactly.
