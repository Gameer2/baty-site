Multiple Integrals — Double Integrals over a Region

A double integral over a planar region R is two ordinary integrals, nested: integrate first across the region at a fixed outer value, then integrate that result across the outer range. Works over a Type I Cartesian region (y between two curves of x) or a polar region (r between two curves of θ, with the extra factor of r in the area element applied automatically). Every bound is evaluated by substitution — never nerdamer's unreliable defint — and the exact result is checked against an independent nested-Simpson numeric double integration before it's shown.
