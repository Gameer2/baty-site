Vector Calculus

The three operations that turn a 2D vector field F = ⟨P(x,y), Q(x,y)⟩ into a number: the divergence and curl (∇·F = P_x + Q_y, ∇×F = Q_x − P_y) with a conservative test and, when the curl vanishes, a potential φ recovered by integration; the line integral of F along a parametric curve, giving the work ∫F·dr and the flux ∫F·n ds; and Green's theorem, computed both as the boundary line integral and as the double integral of Q_x − P_y — the theorem is confirmed by the two sides agreeing, not by trusting either one. Every quantity is checked against central differences or an independent Simpson integration before it is shown.
