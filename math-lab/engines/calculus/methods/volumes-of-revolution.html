Volumes of Revolution — Disk, Washer & Shell

A region under a curve, swept around an axis, traces out a solid. The disk method stacks circular slices perpendicular to the axis; the washer method hollows them out with a second curve; the shell method builds cylindrical shells parallel to the axis. Each volume is computed exactly — antiderivative, then π kept symbolic through the bound substitution — and the result is checked against an independent Simpson integration of the integrand before it's shown, so a π that the CAS quietly turned into a rational approximation is caught, not displayed.
