Parametric & Polar Coordinates

Curves that don't pass the vertical-line test — a point traced as a parameter runs. Parametric form (x(t), y(t)) gives slope dy/dx = y′/x′, arc length L = ∫√(x′²+y′²) dt, and area A = ∫ y·x′ dt; polar form r(θ) gives area A = ½∫r² dθ and arc length L = ∫√(r²+r′²) dθ. The engine differentiates exactly, integrates with π kept symbolic (the unit circle closes to 2π, not a rational that approximates it), and reports each quantity independently — a vertical tangent at the midpoint refuses the slope while the area still resolves, and a cardioid's non-elementary arc length is refused while its 3π/2 area lands.
