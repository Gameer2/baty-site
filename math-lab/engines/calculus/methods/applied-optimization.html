Applied Optimization

The classic "minimize the material" / "maximize the enclosed area" word problem, reduced to its Calc-1 mechanics: on a closed interval, the Extreme Value Theorem guarantees the maximum or minimum sits at a critical point (f′ = 0) or at one of the two endpoints — so every candidate is evaluated and the best one wins. This is the single-variable, closed-form treatment — the general n-variable numerical case (gradient descent, simplex) lives elsewhere.
