Applications of Integration

Arc Length & Surface Area

The two geometric applications of the integral that run on the same engine: the length of a curve L = ∫√(1+(f′)²) dx, and the area of the surface it sweeps when revolved about the x-axis S = 2π∫f√(1+(f′)²) dx. The antiderivative is found exactly, the bounds substituted with π kept symbolic, and every answer is checked against an independent Simpson integration of the integrand before it is shown — so a length the CAS quietly turned into a rational approximation of π is caught, and a length with no elementary closed form (an elliptic integral, like sin x) is refused honestly rather than faked.
