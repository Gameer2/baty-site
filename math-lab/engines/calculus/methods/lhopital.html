Limits & Derivatives

L'Hôpital's Rule

For a quotient f(x)/g(x) that substitutes to the indeterminate form 0/0 or ∞/∞, the limit equals lim f′(x)/g′(x) — differentiate top and bottom separately, not the quotient rule. This page's entire point is the check: a quotient that isn't indeterminate at the point is refused here, even when its limit is easy to find some other way, because applying the rule where it isn't needed is exactly the mistake this page exists to prevent.
