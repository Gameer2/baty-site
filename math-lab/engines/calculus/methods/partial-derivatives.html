Partial Derivatives, Gradient & Tangent Planes

The first genuinely two-variable idea: f(x, y) as a surface, the partials f_x and f_y as slopes in the coordinate directions, the gradient ∇f as the direction of steepest ascent, and the tangent plane that kisses the surface at a point. Each piece is exact, and the symbolic partials are checked against central-difference approximations before they're shown — so a diff() the CAS gets wrong on a transcendental form is caught, not displayed.
