Limits

The first idea in the subject: what f(x) approaches as x closes in on a point — which is not the same as what f is at that point, and need not exist at all. Solved exactly, with the derivation shown: direct substitution where the function is continuous, L'Hôpital's Rule for 0/0 and ∞/∞, and both sides checked before any answer is reported.
