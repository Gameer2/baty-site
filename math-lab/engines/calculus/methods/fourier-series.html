Fourier Series

Give a function f(x) on an interval and the engine writes its Fourier series — full [−L, L], half-range sine, or half-range cosine — computing every coefficient by Simpson's rule and keeping the exact π-symbolic form wherever the integral is elementary. The N-term partial sum is then plotted against f so the convergence (and the Gibbs overshoot at a jump) is plain to see.
