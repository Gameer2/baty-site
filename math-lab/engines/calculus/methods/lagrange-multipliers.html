Lagrange Multipliers — Constrained Optimization

Maximize or minimize f(x, y) while confined to a constraint curve g(x, y) = c: at the optimum, the level curves of f and the constraint curve are tangent, which means ∇f = λ∇g for some scalar λ. Solving that system exactly turned out to be untrustworthy — nerdamer's own system solver returns an inexact, incomplete answer even on the textbook circle example — so this method solves it with a multi-start numeric search instead, and verifies every candidate independently: the directional derivative of f along the constraint curve itself must vanish there.
