Related Rates

Differentiate an implicit relationship between quantities that depend on time, substitute the known values and rates, and solve for the unknown rate — the ladder-down-a-wall, expanding-balloon, melting-snowball chapter. The engine builds the chain rule by hand (d/dt[f] = Σ ∂f/∂v · dv/dt, since a CAS treats every other symbol as a constant), recovers the rate without trusting solve() (which quietly turns π into a rational), and verifies the answer against an independent numeric total-derivative residual that must vanish at the instant.
