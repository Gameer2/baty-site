Power Series & Radius of Convergence

For ∑ cₙ(x−a)ⁿ, finds the radius of convergence R from the coefficients (ratio test, root test fallback), classifies both endpoints x = a ± R by reusing the convergence decision tree, and assembles the interval — including R = ∞ (converges everywhere) and R = 0 (converges only at the centre). Checked against the partial sums inside and outside the radius before it is shown.
