Root Finding

Müller's Method

Fits a parabola through the last three iterates and jumps to its nearest root — x_3 = x_2 − 2c / (b + sgn(b)·√(b²−4ac)) — no derivative needed, order ≈ 1.84, and (in principle) able to find complex roots.
