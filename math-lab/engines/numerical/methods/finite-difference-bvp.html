Finite-Difference BVP

Discretizes the whole interval at once by replacing derivatives with central differences at each interior node — -(1 + h/2·p_i)w_{i-1} + (2 + h²·q_i)w_i - (1 - h/2·p_i)w_{i+1} = -h²·r_i — producing a tridiagonal linear system solved in one shot, no iteration.
