Hermite Interpolation

Builds the unique degree ≤ 2n+1 polynomial through n+1 nodes by matching both the value f(xᵢ) and the slope f′(xᵢ) at every node — smoother than Lagrange through the same points, and exact for any polynomial up to that degree.
