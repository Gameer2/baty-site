Romberg Integration

Applies Richardson extrapolation to a sequence of composite Trapezoidal-rule estimates at h, h/2, h/4, … — R(i,j) = R(i,j−1) + [R(i,j−1) − R(i−1,j−1)] / (4^j − 1) — cancelling the leading error term at each level so the diagonal converges far faster than plain Trapezoidal or Simpson's at the same base panel count.
