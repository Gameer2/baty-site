Boundary Value Problems

Shooting Method

Turns the linear BVP y'' = p(x)y' + q(x)y + r(x), y(a)=α, y(b)=β into two initial value problems — a particular one with y₁'(a)=0 and a homogeneous one with y₂(a)=0, y₂'(a)=1 — integrates both with RK4 and combines them linearly as y = y₁ + c·y₂ to hit the far boundary exactly.
